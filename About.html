"Future Farms. Smarter Lands. Better Biomes."

About AGED

AGED is a youth-driven agri-tech initiative rethinking the way we farm, feed, and flourish. With a mission to equip farmers and communities with cutting-edge tools, AGED brings the power of AI, innovation, and community to agriculture in Africa.

History

AGED started as a dream — one driven by firsthand experiences of the limitations in current farming systems, particularly in rural communities across Nigeria. From unreliable access to agronomic support, to data being nonexistent or underutilized, AGED emerged with the goal of turning that gap into opportunity. Born in Bwari, Nigeria, it now grows as a blueprint for youth-led innovation in agriculture, where each field, soil type, and plant health can be digitally understood and acted upon.

Our Vision

To cultivate a digitally-enabled agricultural ecosystem where every farm — no matter how small — is intelligent, efficient, and connected to a wider biome of support, sustainability, and growth.

Our Mission

To empower youth and farmers with smart tools, AI-driven solutions, and collaborative infrastructure that revolutionize food systems, enhance productivity, and build resilience in Africa's lands and livelihoods.

What We Do

AI-Powered Plant & Soil Diagnostics

Users scan plant or soil images to receive AI-generated diagnoses — including pest infections, nutrient deficiencies, and crop health assessments — all in seconds.

Smart Record-Keeping & Logistics

From input tracking to delivery routes, farmers can manage inventory, link with buyers, and plan efficiently — all in one mobile-accessible space.

GPS-Based Risk & Crop Mapping

Our system uses real-time map layers to suggest crop choices, detect flood/drought risk, and guide farmers on what grows best — exactly where they are.

Home Hub & Innovation Access

Users stay connected to AGED's core team for training, tool upgrades, agricultural resources, and a vibrant community of innovators solving real agro problems.
